Console emulator

Runs SNES, Game Boy Advance and Nintendo 64 games in your browser using EmulatorJS. No games are included — pick a ROM file you legally own. It stays on your device.

  • Multiplayer: plug in up to four gamepads before starting; map them in the emulator menu (⚙).
  • Keyboard: arrows, Z/X/A/S and Enter work by default; rebind via ⚙ → Control Settings.
  • Saves and save states stay in your browser. N64 is demanding and works best in Chrome/Edge on a recent PC.
  • Emulator: EmulatorJS (GPL-3.0). Cores: snes9x, mGBA, Mupen64Plus-Next — licenses in emulator/CORE-LICENSES.
